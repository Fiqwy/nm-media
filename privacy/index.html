Privacy Policy

NM Media · Gold Coast, Queensland · Last updated 29 October 2026

NM Media is a small Gold Coast business run by Nick Matthews. I build websites for tradies and other local businesses. This policy explains what personal information I collect when you get in touch, what I do with it, and how you can ask me to change or delete it. It is written to follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What I collect

When you respond to one of my ads or fill in a form, I may collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address.
  • The answers you give in the form, such as your trade, what you currently have online, and the best time to call you.
  • Anything you choose to tell me afterwards by phone, text or email about your business and what you are after.

If you enquire through a Facebook or Instagram ad, the form is provided by Meta. Meta pre-fills some details from your profile and passes them to me. Meta handles that information under its own privacy policy.

This page does not use cookies or analytics.

Why I collect it

  • To ring or message you back about the enquiry you made, usually within a few minutes during the day.
  • To prepare for that conversation, for example by looking at what currently comes up when someone searches for your trade in your area.
  • To send you anything you ask for, such as examples of sites I have built or a quote.
  • To keep a simple record of enquiries so I know who I have spoken to and what was agreed.

I do not sell, rent or swap your details with anyone. I do not add you to a marketing list unless you ask to be on one.

Who else sees it

Your details are stored in the tools I use to run the business: Meta's Leads Centre (where form responses arrive), my phone, my email, and a spreadsheet or customer record system. Some of these services store data on servers outside Australia, including in the United States. I only use providers with their own privacy protections, and I only give them what is needed to do the job.

I will only share your information with anyone else if you ask me to, or if the law requires it.

How long I keep it

If we do not end up working together, I delete your enquiry details within 12 months of our last contact, or sooner if you ask. If you become a client, I keep what I need to deliver and support your website, and for tax and record-keeping obligations.

Keeping it safe

I take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access: password-protected accounts, two-factor authentication where available, and access limited to me. No system is perfectly secure, but if something goes wrong that could affect you, I will tell you.

Your choices

  • Ask what I hold. You can ask for a copy of the personal information I have about you.
  • Correct it. If something is wrong or out of date, tell me and I will fix it.
  • Delete it. You can ask me to delete your details at any time, and I will, unless I am legally required to keep a record.
  • Stop contact. Reply STOP to any text, or tell me by phone or email, and I will not contact you again.

Contact me using the details below and I will respond within a few business days.

Complaints

If you think I have mishandled your information, please contact me first so I can put it right. If you are not satisfied with my response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I handle personal information, I will update this page and the date at the top.
